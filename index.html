Chicken Biryani recipe

About Biryani

Chicken biryani is a delicious indian
dish made rice,chicken and spices.
it is full of flavour!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 1 cup of chicken
  • 2 tablespoons of oil
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • 1 teaspoon of coriander

How to Make Biryani

  1. Wash the rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add the chicken. Cook until it is done.
  3. Add the spices and cook for a few more minutes.
  4. Add the cooked chicken to the rice and mix well.
  5. Cook on low heat for 10-15 minutes.

cooking information

cooking time: 30 minutes
servings: 4 people
